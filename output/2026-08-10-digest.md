- **[Headless OAuth Token 401 Bug (Fixed v2.1.225)](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog)** — A transient 401 could replace a long-lived `CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN` with a stored login's short-lived token, breaking all headless sessions until a restart.
